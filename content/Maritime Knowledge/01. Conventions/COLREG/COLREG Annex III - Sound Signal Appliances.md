# COLREG Annex III - Sound Signal Appliances

> Note: This page is a study note, not a verbatim legal copy.
> 주의: 이 페이지는 학습용 정리이며 법령 원문 전체의 재배포본이 아니다.

Official source: USCG Navigation Rules / 72 COLREGS — https://www.navcen.uscg.gov/navigation-rules-amalgamated

## Purpose

Annex III gives technical standards for sound signal appliances.

## Scope

It supports Part D by specifying whistles, bells, gongs, frequencies, ranges, and installation details.

## Core Content

- Whistle range and frequency depend on vessel length.
- Bells and gongs have specified sound characteristics.
- Placement should allow signals to be heard effectively.

## Watch Points

- Part D tells when to signal; Annex III tells what equipment standard supports it.
- Equipment condition matters in restricted visibility.

## Study Order

1. Study Part D signals.
2. Then learn appliance standards.
3. Connect vessel length to sound equipment.
