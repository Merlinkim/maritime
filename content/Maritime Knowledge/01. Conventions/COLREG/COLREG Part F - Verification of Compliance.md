# COLREG Part F - Verification of Compliance

> Note: This page is a study note, not a verbatim legal copy.
> 주의: 이 페이지는 학습용 정리이며 법령 원문 전체의 재배포본이 아니다.

Official source: USCG Navigation Rules / 72 COLREGS — https://www.navcen.uscg.gov/navigation-rules-amalgamated

## Purpose

Part F concerns verification of compliance with the Convention.

## Scope

Rules 39 to 41 address audit and compliance mechanisms.

## Core Content

- States must give effect to compliance verification.
- The part supports consistent implementation of COLREG obligations.
- It is more administrative than ship-handling focused.

## Watch Points

- Usually less central for watchkeeping, but important for convention structure.
- Do not confuse State compliance verification with onboard collision-avoidance decisions.

## Study Order

1. Learn why it was added.
2. Connect to IMO audit/compliance culture.
3. Keep operational rules separate.
