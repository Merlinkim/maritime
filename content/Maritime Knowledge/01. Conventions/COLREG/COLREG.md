# COLREG

> Convention on the International Regulations for Preventing Collisions at Sea, 1972
> 국제해상충돌예방규칙

Official source: USCG Navigation Rules / 72 COLREGS — https://www.navcen.uscg.gov/navigation-rules-amalgamated

## What COLREG Is

COLREG sets the international rules of the road for preventing collisions at sea.
COLREG는 해상에서 충돌을 예방하기 위한 국제 항법 규칙이다.

It must be applied with seamanship, lookout, risk assessment, and early action.
COLREG는 선원의 상무, 경계, 충돌위험 판단, 조기 동작과 함께 적용해야 한다.

## Learning Focus

- Part B is the heart of collision avoidance.
- Rules 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, and 19 are especially important.
- Lights, shapes, and sound signals must be connected to vessel status and risk.
