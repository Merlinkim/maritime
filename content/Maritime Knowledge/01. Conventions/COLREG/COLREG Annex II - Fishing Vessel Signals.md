# COLREG Annex II - Fishing Vessel Signals

> Note: This page is a study note, not a verbatim legal copy.
> 주의: 이 페이지는 학습용 정리이며 법령 원문 전체의 재배포본이 아니다.

Official source: USCG Navigation Rules / 72 COLREGS — https://www.navcen.uscg.gov/navigation-rules-amalgamated

## Purpose

Annex II provides additional signals for fishing vessels fishing in close proximity.

## Scope

It supplements the ordinary lights and shapes for fishing operations.

## Core Content

- Additional signals help identify fishing operations and gear situations.
- Signals differ depending on activity such as trawling, purse seining, or shooting/hauling gear.
- The purpose is to reduce collision and gear-conflict risk.

## Watch Points

- Fishing vessel signals are practical in congested fishing areas.
- Do not assume every vessel with fishing gear has the same maneuverability.

## Study Order

1. Learn basic fishing lights first.
2. Then add Annex II special signals.
3. Connect signals to maneuvering limitations.
