# COLREG Annex I - Lights and Shapes

> Note: This page is a study note, not a verbatim legal copy.
> 주의: 이 페이지는 학습용 정리이며 법령 원문 전체의 재배포본이 아니다.

Official source: USCG Navigation Rules / 72 COLREGS — https://www.navcen.uscg.gov/navigation-rules-amalgamated

## Purpose

Annex I gives technical positioning and details for lights and shapes.

## Scope

It supports Part C by specifying placement, spacing, sectors, screening, and technical details.

## Core Content

- Vertical and horizontal positioning matters.
- Light sectors and visibility ranges must meet technical requirements.
- Shapes must be displayed where best seen.
- Special arrangements may apply for small vessels or particular designs.

## Watch Points

- Part C tells what to show; Annex I tells how it must be arranged.
- Poor placement can make a legally required signal misleading.

## Study Order

1. Study Part C first.
2. Then use Annex I to understand placement.
3. Practice interpreting vessel aspect.
