# COLREG Part C - Lights and Shapes

> Note: This page is a study note, not a verbatim legal copy.
> 주의: 이 페이지는 학습용 정리이며 법령 원문 전체의 재배포본이 아니다.

Official source: USCG Navigation Rules / 72 COLREGS — https://www.navcen.uscg.gov/navigation-rules-amalgamated

## Purpose

Part C sets requirements for navigation lights and day shapes.

## Scope

Rules 20 to 31 identify vessel type, aspect, activity, and status.

## Core Content

- Lights apply from sunset to sunrise and in restricted visibility when required.
- Shapes communicate status by day.
- Different vessel categories show different combinations.
- Aspect recognition helps determine risk and vessel orientation.

## Watch Points

- Do not memorize lights as drawings only; connect each light to what the vessel is doing.
- Fishing, towing, RAM, NUC, CBD, pilot, and anchored/aground vessels are common exam areas.
- Length thresholds affect required lights.

## Study Order

1. Learn basic light colors and arcs.
2. Study power-driven and sailing vessels.
3. Then special statuses and operations.
