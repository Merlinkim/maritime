# COLREG Annex IV - Distress Signals

> Note: This page is a study note, not a verbatim legal copy.
> 주의: 이 페이지는 학습용 정리이며 법령 원문 전체의 재배포본이 아니다.

Official source: USCG Navigation Rules / 72 COLREGS — https://www.navcen.uscg.gov/navigation-rules-amalgamated

## Purpose

Annex IV lists signals indicating distress and need of assistance.

## Scope

It applies when a vessel or person is in grave and imminent danger and requires help.

## Core Content

- Distress signals may be visual, sound, radio, pyrotechnic, electronic, or procedural.
- The meaning is urgent: assistance is required.
- False or careless use creates serious safety consequences.

## Watch Points

- Know distress, urgency, and safety communication distinction.
- Distress signals link COLREG with GMDSS/SAR practice.

## Study Order

1. Learn common visual distress signals.
2. Connect to radio distress procedures.
3. Review SAR response obligations.
