# COLREG Part E - Exemptions

> Note: This page is a study note, not a verbatim legal copy.
> 주의: 이 페이지는 학습용 정리이며 법령 원문 전체의 재배포본이 아니다.

Official source: USCG Navigation Rules / 72 COLREGS — https://www.navcen.uscg.gov/navigation-rules-amalgamated

## Purpose

Part E contains exemption provisions for certain vessels whose construction predates changes.

## Scope

Rule 38 addresses limited exemptions from technical requirements.

## Core Content

- Exemptions relate mainly to lights, shapes, and sound-signal appliance technical details.
- They do not exempt a vessel from collision-avoidance responsibility.
- Applicability depends on date, construction, and specified requirement.

## Watch Points

- Do not use exemptions as a conduct-rule escape.
- Operational responsibility remains.

## Study Order

1. Understand purpose.
2. Connect to technical equipment requirements.
3. Keep separate from steering and sailing rules.
