# COLREG Part D - Sound and Light Signals

> 주의: 이 페이지는 학습용 정리이며 법령 원문 전체의 재배포본이 아니다.

Official source: USCG Navigation Rules / 72 COLREGS — https://www.navcen.uscg.gov/navigation-rules-amalgamated

## Purpose

Part D sets maneuvering, warning, and restricted-visibility sound signals.

## Scope

Rules 32 to 37 cover definitions of whistles/bells/gongs and when signals are used.

## Core Content

- Short and prolonged blasts have defined meanings.
- Maneuvering signals apply when vessels are in sight.
- Warning signals communicate doubt or danger.
- Restricted-visibility signals identify vessel status and movement.
- Distress signals are listed separately in Annex IV.

## Watch Points

- Do not mix maneuvering signals with restricted-visibility signals.
- Sound signals support but do not replace safe action.
- Know when light signals may supplement whistle signals.

## Study Order

1. Learn sound-signal vocabulary.
2. Separate in-sight from restricted visibility.
3. Practice vessel-status signal recognition.
