# COLREG Part A - General

> Note: This page is a study note, not a verbatim legal copy.
> 주의: 이 페이지는 학습용 정리이며 법령 원문 전체의 재배포본이 아니다.

Official source: USCG Navigation Rules / 72 COLREGS — https://www.navcen.uscg.gov/navigation-rules-amalgamated

## Purpose

Part A defines application, responsibility, and key terms.

## Scope

Rules 1 to 3 set the foundation for all later rules.

## Core Content

- Rule 1 explains where the rules apply.
- Rule 2 preserves responsibility for good seamanship and special circumstances.
- Rule 3 defines key vessel and navigation terms.

## Watch Points

- Rule 2 is critical: compliance with the rules does not excuse poor seamanship.
- Definitions drive the rest of the rules; do not skip them.
- Understand vessel categories such as power-driven, sailing, fishing, NUC, RAM, CBD, and seaplane/WIG where applicable.

## Study Order

1. Learn definitions first.
2. Then study responsibility.
3. Apply definitions to lights and conduct rules.
