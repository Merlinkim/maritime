# MARPOL Annex I - Oil

Official source: IMO MARPOL convention page — https://www.imo.org/en/about/conventions/pages/international-convention-for-the-prevention-of-pollution-from-ships-%28marpol%29.aspx

## Purpose

Annex I prevents pollution by oil from operational discharges and accidental releases.

## Scope

It applies to oil tankers and other ships carrying or using oil, with requirements depending on ship type and tonnage.

## Core Content

- Oil Record Book records relevant oil operations.
- Oily water separators and oil filtering equipment control machinery-space bilge discharge.
- Oil tankers have cargo-area controls, slop arrangements, and discharge monitoring requirements.
- Special areas impose stricter discharge controls.

## Watch Points

- Know the difference between machinery-space oil and cargo oil.
- Recordkeeping is a compliance issue, not a clerical extra.
- Illegal discharge often involves bypasses, false entries, or poor maintenance.

## Study Order

1. Study definitions and discharge logic.
2. Learn Oil Record Book entries.
3. Connect equipment to permitted discharge conditions.
