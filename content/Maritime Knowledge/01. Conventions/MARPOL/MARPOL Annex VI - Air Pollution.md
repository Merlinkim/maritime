# MARPOL Annex VI - Air Pollution

> 주의: 이 페이지는 학습용 정리이며 법령 원문 전체의 재배포본이 아니다.

Official source: IMO MARPOL convention page — https://www.imo.org/en/about/conventions/pages/international-convention-for-the-prevention-of-pollution-from-ships-%28marpol%29.aspx

## Purpose

Annex VI controls air pollution and greenhouse-gas-related measures from ships.

## Scope

It covers ozone-depleting substances, NOx, SOx, particulate matter, VOCs, incineration, fuel oil quality, and energy efficiency/carbon intensity measures.

## Core Content

- Sulphur limits apply globally and more strictly in Emission Control Areas.
- NOx limits depend on engine tier and operating area.
- Fuel oil documentation, bunker delivery notes, and sampling support enforcement.
- EEDI/EEXI/CII and SEEMP connect design and operational efficiency.

## Watch Points

- Annex VI changes frequently compared with older pollution rules.
- Fuel changeover, scrubbers, samples, and records are inspection-sensitive.
- Carbon intensity measures are now part of operational management.

## Study Order

1. Start with SOx and fuel controls.
2. Then learn NOx tiers.
3. Finish with EEXI/CII/SEEMP concepts.
