## Purpose

Annex IV controls pollution by sewage from ships.

## Scope

It applies to ships that meet the annex's size, voyage, and personnel thresholds.

## Core Content

- Ships may use sewage treatment plants, comminuting/disinfecting systems, or holding tanks.
- Discharge restrictions depend on treatment status and distance from land.
- International Sewage Pollution Prevention Certificate may be required.

## Watch Points

- Know treated vs untreated sewage logic.
- Special area requirements can be stricter.
- Maintenance of treatment plant affects compliance.

## Study Order

1. Learn system types.
2. Study discharge distance logic.
3. Connect certificate and inspections.
