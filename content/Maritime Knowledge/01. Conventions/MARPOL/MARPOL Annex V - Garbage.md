## Purpose

Annex V controls garbage pollution from ships.

## Scope

It applies broadly and is one of the most practical daily MARPOL annexes.

## Core Content

- Garbage is categorized for handling and discharge control.
- Plastics are subject to very strict prohibition.
- Garbage Management Plan, placards, and Garbage Record Book support compliance.
- Special areas impose tighter restrictions.

## Watch Points

- Garbage rules are operational and crew-behaviour dependent.
- Food waste, cargo residues, cooking oil, plastics, and domestic waste are treated differently.
- Accurate segregation onboard prevents later non-compliance.

## Study Order

1. Learn garbage categories.
2. Study what can never be discharged.
3. Practice Garbage Record Book scenarios.
