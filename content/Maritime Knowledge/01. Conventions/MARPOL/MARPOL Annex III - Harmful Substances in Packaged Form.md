# MARPOL Annex III - Harmful Substances in Packaged Form

> Note: This page is a study note, not a verbatim legal copy.

Official source: IMO MARPOL convention page — https://www.imo.org/en/about/conventions/pages/international-convention-for-the-prevention-of-pollution-from-ships-%28marpol%29.aspx

## Purpose

Annex III prevents pollution by harmful substances carried by sea in packaged form.

## Scope

It applies to packaged dangerous/harmful goods identified as marine pollutants.

## Core Content

- Packaging, marking, labelling, documentation, stowage, and quantity limitations are key controls.
- The IMDG Code is the practical reference for packaged dangerous goods.
- Marine pollutant identification must be reflected in documentation and marks.

## Watch Points

- Annex III is closely tied to IMDG Code practice.
- Incorrect declaration can defeat the entire control system.
- Packaged form differs from bulk cargo regulation.

## Study Order

1. Study IMDG classes and marine pollutant mark.
2. Learn documentation flow.
3. Review segregation and stowage.
