# MARPOL Annex II - Noxious Liquid Substances in Bulk

## Purpose

Annex II controls pollution from noxious liquid substances carried in bulk.

## Scope

It mainly applies to chemical tankers and ships carrying liquid chemical cargoes in bulk.

## Core Content

- Substances are categorized by pollution hazard.
- Cargo stripping, tank washing, prewash, and discharge controls depend on category and port facilities.
- Procedures and Arrangements Manual guides shipboard handling.
- Cargo Record Book documents operations.

## Watch Points

- Annex II is category-driven; do not memorize one discharge rule for all cargoes.
- Port reception facilities and prewash requirements matter.
- Connect Annex II with the IBC Code.

## Study Order

1. Learn pollution categories.
2. Study stripping/washing/discharge sequence.
3. Connect records to cargo operation.
