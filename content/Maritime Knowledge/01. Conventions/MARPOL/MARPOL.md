# MARPOL

> International Convention for the Prevention of Pollution from Ships
> 선박으로부터의 오염방지를 위한 국제협약

Official source: IMO MARPOL convention page — https://www.imo.org/en/about/conventions/pages/international-convention-for-the-prevention-of-pollution-from-ships-%28marpol%29.aspx

## What MARPOL Is

MARPOL is the main international convention for preventing pollution from ships.
MARPOL은 선박 기인 오염을 방지하기 위한 대표 국제협약이다.

It is organized by pollutant type through Annexes.
오염물질 종류별로 Annex를 나누어 규제한다.

## Learning Focus

- Annex I and VI are usually the most frequently encountered in general ship operation.
- Annex V is practical for daily garbage handling.
- Annex II and III are cargo-specific.
- Annex IV depends on sewage system, ship type, and trading area.
