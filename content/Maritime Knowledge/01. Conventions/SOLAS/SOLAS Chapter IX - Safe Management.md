## Purpose

Chapter IX makes the International Safety Management Code mandatory.

## Scope

It applies management-system thinking to ship operation and company responsibility.

## Core Content

- The ISM Code requires a safety management system.
- The company must define responsibilities, procedures, reporting, maintenance, and emergency preparedness.
- Document of Compliance and Safety Management Certificate are central documents.
- Non-conformities and corrective actions drive improvement.

## Watch Points

- ISM is about how the company controls risk, not only onboard paperwork.
- DP operations depend heavily on management systems, competence, maintenance, and MOC.
- Audits test whether practice matches procedure.

## Study Order

1. Learn SMS structure.
2. Connect certificates to company/ship.
3. Apply ISM to incidents and near misses.
