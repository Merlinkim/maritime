## Purpose

Chapter III regulates life-saving appliances and arrangements, including lifeboats, liferafts, rescue boats, muster arrangements, drills, and maintenance.

## Scope

It covers equipment and operational readiness for abandonment and rescue.

## Core Content

- LSA must be properly carried, located, marked, maintained, and ready for immediate use.
- Muster lists and emergency instructions assign duties.
- Drills build crew familiarity with emergency roles.
- Launching arrangements are as important as the survival craft itself.

## Watch Points

- LSA failures often come from maintenance and launching arrangements, not only missing equipment.
- Know the difference between lifeboat, liferaft, rescue boat, and fast rescue boat.
- Drill records matter during inspection.

## Study Order

1. Start with abandonment sequence.
2. Then learn each appliance type.
3. Finish with drills, maintenance, and records.
