## Purpose

Chapter II-1 covers structural, machinery, electrical, subdivision, damage stability, steering gear, and related construction safety.

## Scope

It mainly applies to ship design and essential engineering systems for survivability.

## Core Content

- Subdivision and damage stability reduce the chance that flooding leads to loss of ship.
- Machinery and electrical systems must support safe operation and emergency conditions.
- Steering gear requirements protect maneuverability after failures.
- Watertight integrity and openings are central to survivability.

## Watch Points

- This chapter is highly technical and connects directly to naval architecture.
- For DP vessels, power generation, switchboards, redundancy, and emergency power concepts link strongly to DP FMEA.
- Damage stability is not the same as intact stability.

## Study Order

1. Review subdivision first.
2. Then study machinery/electrical redundancy.
3. Finish with watertight integrity and steering gear.
