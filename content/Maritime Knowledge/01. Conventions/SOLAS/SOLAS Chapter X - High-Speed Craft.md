# SOLAS Chapter X - High-Speed Craft

> Note: This page is a study note, not a verbatim legal copy.
> 주의: 이 페이지는 학습용 정리이며 법령 원문 전체의 재배포본이 아니다.

Official source: IMO SOLAS convention page — https://www.imo.org/en/about/conventions/pages/international-convention-for-the-safety-of-life-at-sea-%28solas%29%2C-1974.aspx

## Purpose

Chapter X makes the High-Speed Craft Code mandatory for qualifying craft.

## Scope

It applies to craft whose design and operation differ from conventional displacement ships.

## Core Content

- HSC safety relies on design, operational limitations, route assumptions, and evacuation arrangements.
- Certification is tied to the HSC Code.
- Crew training and operating permits are important.

## Watch Points

- High-speed craft safety is route and operation dependent.
- Do not apply conventional ship assumptions blindly.

## Study Order

1. Understand HSC concept.
2. Study operational limits.
3. Connect certification to route risk.
