## Purpose

Chapter VI covers safe carriage of cargoes other than dangerous goods, including cargo information, stowage, securing, bulk cargo precautions, and grain.

## Scope

It applies to cargo safety and ship stability risks arising from cargo.

## Core Content

- Cargo information must be available before loading.
- Cargo must be stowed and secured to prevent shifting and structural damage.
- Bulk cargoes can create hazards such as liquefaction, oxygen depletion, and chemical danger.
- Grain cargo has special stability concerns.

## Watch Points

- Cargo safety is not only a cargo officer issue; it affects stability and hull stress.
- IMSBC Code and Cargo Securing Manual are key connected documents.
- Incorrect cargo information can become a major casualty factor.

## Study Order

1. Learn cargo information flow.
2. Study securing and shifting.
3. Connect bulk cargo hazards to stability.
