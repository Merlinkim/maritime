## Purpose

Chapter XIV makes the Polar Code safety provisions mandatory for ships operating in polar waters.

## Scope

It covers ship design, equipment, operation, planning, and environmental hazards in polar regions.

## Core Content

- Polar hazards include ice, low temperature, remoteness, limited charting, communication limits, and rescue difficulty.
- Polar Ship Certificate and Polar Water Operational Manual are key documents.
- Operational assessment must match ship capability and route conditions.

## Watch Points

- Polar operation is risk-based and capability-based.
- Do not treat ordinary open-water assumptions as valid in ice/remote areas.

## Study Order

1. Study polar hazards.
2. Learn certificate/manual relationship.
3. Connect voyage planning to rescue limitations.
