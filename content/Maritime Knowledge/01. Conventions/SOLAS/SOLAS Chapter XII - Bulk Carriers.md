## Purpose

Chapter XII gives additional safety measures for bulk carriers.

## Scope

It addresses structural vulnerability and cargo-related risks specific to bulk carriers.

## Core Content

- Bulk carriers face risks from cargo density, hold flooding, structural stress, corrosion, and loading patterns.
- Additional structural and survey measures apply.
- Loading instruments and cargo information support safe operation.

## Watch Points

- Bulk carrier casualties historically drove this chapter.
- Study cargo density, hold condition, and loading stress together.

## Study Order

1. Review bulk carrier casualty causes.
2. Study structural/survey measures.
3. Connect cargo operations with hull girder stress.
