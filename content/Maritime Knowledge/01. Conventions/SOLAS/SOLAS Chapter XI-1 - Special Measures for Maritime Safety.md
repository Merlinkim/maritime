# SOLAS Chapter XI-1 - Special Measures for Maritime Safety

> Note: This page is a study note, not a verbatim legal copy.
> 주의: 이 페이지는 학습용 정리이며 법령 원문 전체의 재배포본이 아니다.

Official source: IMO SOLAS convention page — https://www.imo.org/en/about/conventions/pages/international-convention-for-the-safety-of-life-at-sea-%28solas%29%2C-1974.aspx

## Purpose

Chapter XI-1 contains special safety measures such as enhanced surveys, ship identification number, continuous synopsis record, and related controls.

## Scope

It strengthens traceability, survey, and safety oversight.

## Core Content

- Ship identification supports traceability.
- Continuous Synopsis Record records key administrative history.
- Enhanced survey requirements improve structural safety oversight for certain ship types.

## Watch Points

- CSR and IMO number are common inspection items.
- This chapter is safety-focused; Chapter XI-2 is security-focused.

## Study Order

1. Separate XI-1 from XI-2.
2. Study IMO number and CSR.
3. Connect enhanced surveys to high-risk ship types.
