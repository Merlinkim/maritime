# SOLAS

> International Convention for the Safety of Life at Sea
> 해상인명안전협약

Official source: IMO SOLAS convention page — https://www.imo.org/en/about/conventions/pages/international-convention-for-the-safety-of-life-at-sea-%28solas%29%2C-1974.aspx

## What SOLAS Is

SOLAS is the main international convention for the safety of merchant ships.
SOLAS는 상선의 안전 기준을 정하는 대표적인 국제협약이다.

It sets minimum standards for construction, equipment, operation, emergency systems, navigation safety, security, audits, polar operations, and industrial personnel.
선박 구조, 설비, 운항, 비상대응, 항해안전, 보안, 감사, 극지 운항, 산업인력 수송 등에 대한 최소 기준을 둔다.

## Learning Focus

- Chapter II-1, II-2, III, IV, V are the practical safety core.
- Chapter IX links SOLAS to the ISM Code.
- Chapter XI-2 links SOLAS to the ISPS Code.
- Chapter XIV links SOLAS to the Polar Code.
- Chapter XV is important for offshore and industrial personnel transfer context.
