## Purpose

Chapter V covers navigational safety requirements, including voyage planning, navigational equipment, bridge procedures, danger messages, and assistance obligations.

## Scope

Unlike many SOLAS chapters, Chapter V has broad relevance across ship types and navigation practice.

## Core Content

- Voyage planning must cover berth-to-berth risk.
- Ships must carry appropriate navigational equipment.
- Masters have obligations relating to distress assistance and danger reporting.
- Bridge procedures, lookout, safe speed, and navigation records connect SOLAS with COLREG and STCW practice.

## Watch Points

- This chapter is essential for officers of the watch.
- ECDIS, AIS, VDR, BNWAS, radar, gyro, and charts are practical exam areas.
- SOLAS V supports professional judgment; it does not replace COLREG.
