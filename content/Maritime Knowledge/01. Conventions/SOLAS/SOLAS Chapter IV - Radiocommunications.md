# SOLAS Chapter IV - Radiocommunications

> Note: This page is a study note, not a verbatim legal copy.

## Purpose

Chapter IV makes the GMDSS framework mandatory for ships subject to SOLAS.

## Scope

It covers distress alerting, search and rescue communications, safety information, and radio equipment carriage.

## Core Content

- GMDSS divides sea areas by available radio coverage.
- Ships must be able to send and receive distress alerts.
- Radio equipment requirements depend on sea area.
- Watchkeeping and maintenance arrangements support continuous availability.

## Watch Points

- Understand Sea Area A1, A2, A3, and A4.
- Distress alerting is not only voice; DSC, satellite, and MSI matter.
- GMDSS links directly to SAR response.

## Study Order

1. Learn sea areas.
2. Map each area to equipment.
3. Practice distress, urgency, and safety communication logic.
