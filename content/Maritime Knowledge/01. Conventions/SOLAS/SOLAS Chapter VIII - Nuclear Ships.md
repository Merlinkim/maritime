# SOLAS Chapter VIII - Nuclear Ships

> Note: This page is a study note, not a verbatim legal copy.
> 주의: 이 페이지는 학습용 정리이며 법령 원문 전체의 재배포본이 아니다.

Official source: IMO SOLAS convention page — https://www.imo.org/en/about/conventions/pages/international-convention-for-the-safety-of-life-at-sea-%28solas%29%2C-1974.aspx

## Purpose

Chapter VIII addresses safety requirements for nuclear ships.

## Scope

It is specialized and applies only to ships using nuclear power.

## Core Content

- Requires special safety assessment and documentation.
- Addresses radiation hazards and operational controls.
- Connects ship safety with nuclear safety oversight.

## Watch Points

- Rare in ordinary merchant shipping study, but useful as a SOLAS chapter map item.
- Do not confuse nuclear ships with carriage of radioactive cargo under Chapter VII/INF Code.

## Study Order

1. Understand why it exists.
2. Separate nuclear propulsion from radioactive cargo.
3. Know it is specialized.
