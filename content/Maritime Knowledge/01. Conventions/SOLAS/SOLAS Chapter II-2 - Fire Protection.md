## Purpose

Chapter II-2 covers fire prevention, detection, containment, extinction, escape, and structural fire protection.

## Scope

It applies to fire safety arrangements, equipment, procedures, and design philosophy.

## Core Content

- Fire safety objectives are prevention, early detection, containment, suppression, and safe escape.
- Structural divisions limit spread of fire and smoke.
- Fire detection and alarm systems give early warning.
- Fixed and portable firefighting systems must match the hazard.
- Escape routes and emergency lighting support evacuation.

## Watch Points

- Fire zones, A/B class divisions, and machinery space requirements are exam-heavy.
- Do not study equipment names only; study what hazard each system controls.
- Fire safety is linked to drills, maintenance, and crew familiarity.

## Study Order

1. Learn fire triangle and fire classes.
2. Study detection and fixed systems.
3. Connect layout to escape and muster.
