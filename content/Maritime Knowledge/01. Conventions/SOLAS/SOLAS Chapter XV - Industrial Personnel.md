## Purpose

Chapter XV covers ships carrying industrial personnel and makes the IP Code mandatory for relevant ships.

## Scope

It is important for offshore, wind, energy, and specialized industrial operations.

## Core Content

- Industrial personnel are neither ordinary passengers nor crew in the traditional sense.
- The IP Code addresses training, transfer, accommodation, life-saving, and operational safety.
- Applies to ships carrying industrial personnel subject to the chapter's thresholds and definitions.

## Watch Points

- This chapter matters for offshore DP and construction support contexts.
- Clarify whether persons onboard are crew, passengers, special personnel, or industrial personnel.
