## Purpose

Chapter I sets the administrative framework of SOLAS: surveys, certificates, control, casualties, and general obligations.

## Scope

Applies the convention through flag State certification and port State recognition.

## Core Content

- Surveys and inspections verify that the ship complies with SOLAS.
- Certificates provide documentary evidence of compliance.
- Control provisions allow authorities to act when a ship is clearly unsafe.
- Casualty reporting supports safety learning and regulatory improvement.

## Watch Points

- Do not treat a certificate as a guarantee that the ship is safe today.
- Know the difference between flag State responsibility and port State control.
- Survey scope depends on ship type, tonnage, service, and applicable chapter.

## Study Order

1. Understand survey/certificate logic.
2. Connect certificates to onboard document control.
3. Review how deficiencies affect operation.
