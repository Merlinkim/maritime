## Purpose

This section covers how DP operations should be planned, checked, and carried out.

## Core Points

- Before DP operation, the system should be checked against vessel-specific procedures.
- Operations should be planned considering equipment class, environmental limits, activity risk, and failure consequences.
- Operational documentation such as CAMO/TAM/ASOG/WSOG supports decision-making.
- Position loss risk must be managed continuously during the operation.

## Practical DP Meaning

- Operational requirements are where paper DP class becomes real risk control.
- A capable vessel can still be unsafe if configuration, limits, or procedures are wrong.
- DPO decisions should be tied to activity-specific limits and escape routes.
