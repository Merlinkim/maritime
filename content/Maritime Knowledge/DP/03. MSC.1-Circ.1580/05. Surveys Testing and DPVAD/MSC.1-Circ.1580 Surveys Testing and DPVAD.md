## Purpose

This section explains verification through survey, testing, trials, and documentation.

## Core Points

- Initial and periodic verification should confirm the DP system matches the intended class and documentation.
- FMEA and proving trials are central evidence for fault tolerance.
- DPVAD records the DP verification and acceptance basis.
- Testing should be realistic enough to demonstrate failure response.

## Practical DP Meaning

- A DP vessel is not proven by drawings alone.
- FMEA proving trials should test the assumptions, not just demonstrate normal operation.
- Documentation must stay aligned with modifications and actual configuration.
