Reference source: IMO MSC.1/Circ.1580 public copy via USCG — https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/OCSNCOE/References/DP-Guidance/IMO/MSC.1-Circ.1580.pdf

## Purpose

The power system supplies electrical or mechanical power required for DP operation.

## Core Points

- Prime movers, generators, switchboards, bus-ties, transformers, UPS where relevant, fuel/lube/cooling/air starting support, protection systems, and power management are part of the power picture.
- Its DP purpose is not simply to produce power, but to keep enough usable power available after expected failures.
- For DP2/DP3 thinking, independence and fault containment are as important as installed kW.

## Practical DP Meaning

- Blackout prevention, load sharing, PMS behavior, bus configuration, hidden common-mode failures, and auxiliary failures are key watch points.
- Power failure is often the initiating event behind loss of position.
