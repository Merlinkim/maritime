## Purpose

This page defines what a DP FMEA is and what it should cover.

## Core Points

- Identify the DP system boundary.
- List functions, equipment, dependencies, and interfaces.
- Analyze credible failure modes and their effects.
- Connect findings to redundancy, WCFDI, alarms, procedures, and proving trials.

## Practical DP Meaning

- A narrow FMEA misses common-mode failures.
- Auxiliary systems are often as important as headline DP equipment.
- Scope must match the vessel's actual DP notation and operation.
