## Purpose

This page focuses on identifying failure modes and tracing their effects.

## Core Points

- Failure modes include fail-stop, fail-run, drift, wrong signal, loss of feedback, degraded performance, hidden failure, and common-mode failure.
- Effects should be traced from local equipment to sub-system, DP function, and vessel position keeping.
- Detection and alarm quality matter because operators respond to what they can see.
- Mitigation can be automatic, procedural, design-based, or operational.

## Practical DP Meaning

- The dangerous case is often a plausible wrong signal, not a clean trip.
- Effects must be described in operational language.
- Ask what the DPO sees, what the vessel does, and what time is available.
