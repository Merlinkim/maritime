## Purpose

This page explains redundancy and the Worst Case Failure Design Intent.

## Core Points

- Redundancy means the vessel can tolerate specified failures without unacceptable loss of DP capability.
- WCFDI states the worst failure the vessel is designed to withstand.
- The worst case may be a switchboard, compartment, bus section, control group, or support system depending on design.
- WCFDI should be clear enough for operators to understand post-failure capability.

## Practical DP Meaning

- Do not confuse duplicated equipment with true redundancy.
- Shared auxiliaries can defeat redundancy.
- The DPO should know the post-WCF capability, not just the class notation.
