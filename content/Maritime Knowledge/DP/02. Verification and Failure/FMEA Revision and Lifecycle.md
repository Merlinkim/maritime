## Purpose

This page explains why FMEA must be maintained through the vessel lifecycle.

## Core Points

- FMEA should be reviewed after modifications, software updates, major repairs, incidents, near misses, proving trials, and operational changes.
- Document control should make the current accepted version obvious.
- Findings should connect to procedures, spare parts, training, and operational limits.
- Lifecycle management prevents the FMEA from becoming a historical document.

## Practical DP Meaning

- A stale FMEA can be worse than no FMEA because it creates false confidence.
- Management of Change should trigger FMEA review.
- DPOs and engineers should know the practical lessons, not only where the PDF is stored.
