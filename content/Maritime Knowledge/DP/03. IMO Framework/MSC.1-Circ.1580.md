# MSC.1/Circ.1580

> Guidelines for vessels and units with dynamic positioning systems
> 동적 위치유지 시스템을 갖춘 선박 및 유닛에 대한 지침

Reference source: IMO MSC.1/Circ.1580 public copy via USCG — https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Portals/9/OCSNCOE/References/DP-Guidance/IMO/MSC.1-Circ.1580.pdf

## What It Is

MSC.1/Circ.1580 is the IMO guideline that replaced the older MSC/Circ.645 framework for DP systems.
MSC.1/Circ.1580은 기존 MSC/Circ.645 체계를 대체한 IMO의 DP 시스템 지침이다.

It gives design, equipment, operation, testing, documentation, and training guidance for vessels and units using DP.
DP를 사용하는 선박과 유닛에 대해 설계, 장비, 운용, 시험, 문서화, 교육 기준을 제시한다.

## Learning Map

1. Start with definitions and equipment classes.
2. Connect the three sub-systems to redundancy and failure tolerance.
3. Study operational requirements together with CAMO, TAM, ASOG, and WSOG.
4. Use surveys, testing, and DPVAD to understand how compliance is demonstrated.
5. Finish with training and documentation expectations.
