## Purpose

The preamble explains why the updated DP guideline exists and where it fits in IMO safety guidance.

## Core Points

- It was approved by the Maritime Safety Committee.
- It updates previous DP guidance to reflect modern DP practice and technology.
- It is generally aimed at new vessels and units with DP systems.
- It provides a common IMO baseline; flag States, class, clients, and industry guidance may add more detail.

## Practical DP Meaning

- Treat it as the top-level IMO DP reference.
- Do not assume the circular alone is enough for a project; class rules, IMCA guidance, charterer requirements, and flag requirements may also apply.
